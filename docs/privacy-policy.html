Introduction & Overview

About This Policy

Welcome to ToolCEO, a professional file conversion and processing desktop application developed by Noman Rafique. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices — or more precisely, the complete absence of any data collection.

"ToolCEO does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data of any kind, at any time, under any circumstances."

ToolCEO is engineered as a fully offline, self-contained Windows desktop application. All operations — including file conversion, compression, archiving, and format transformation — are executed entirely on your local device using your device's own processing resources.

This policy applies to the ToolCEO desktop application and is intended to meet the requirements of the Microsoft Store, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and general international privacy best practices including GDPR principles.

Plain language summary: ToolCEO works entirely on your computer. Nothing you do inside the app is ever sent to us, to any server, or to any third party. Your files and your work are yours alone.
02

Information We Collect

None

We collect no information whatsoever. The following is an explicit list of data types that ToolCEO does not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
  • Device identifiers, hardware IDs, or machine fingerprints
  • Usage data, telemetry, analytics, or crash reports
  • File names, file contents, or file metadata of any files you process
  • IP addresses, geographic location data, or network information
  • Browser cookies, local storage tokens, or session identifiers
  • Account information, login credentials, or authentication data
  • Payment information or financial data of any kind

ToolCEO operates with no user accounts, no registration, and no login. There is no mechanism within the application to collect or transmit any data because the application has no outbound network connectivity.

We do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind. Files you convert are never uploaded to any server.
03

How We Use Your Information

We Don't

Because ToolCEO collects no information, we use no information. There is no data processing pipeline, no data retention, no data analysis, and no data sharing — because there is no data to work with.

All file processing performed by ToolCEO happens entirely within your device's local environment. When you open a file, convert it, compress it, or export it:

  • The operation runs entirely on your local CPU and memory
  • The output file is saved directly to your chosen local directory
  • No network request is made at any point during the operation
  • No copy of your file is retained by the application after processing
  • No internet connection is required or used — not even for licensing checks
All file processing happens locally on your device. Your documents, images, audio files, and any other content you process with ToolCEO remain exclusively on your machine and are under your complete control at all times.
04

Data Storage & Security

Local Only

ToolCEO stores only the minimal application data required for its own operation, such as user preferences and conversion history, in local application data directories on your Windows device. This data never leaves your device.

Specifically, ToolCEO may store the following locally:

  • Application settings and preferences (stored in your local app data folder)
  • Recent conversion history (file names only, stored locally for your convenience)
  • Tool-specific configuration options saved between sessions

All of the above is stored exclusively on your device and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing the application data folder. None of this information is transmitted externally.

Security model: Because ToolCEO collects no data and operates fully offline, there is no server-side attack surface, no database to breach, and no network transmission to intercept. Your data security is protected by the security of your own Windows device.
05

Third-Party Services

None

ToolCEO integrates with no third-party services of any kind. The application does not use, embed, or call:

  • Analytics platforms (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, etc.)
  • Advertising networks or ad SDKs of any kind
  • Cloud storage or file-sharing services
  • Social media SDKs or login integrations
  • Crash reporting or error tracking services (Sentry, Bugsnag, etc.)
  • Remote APIs, webhooks, or external web services
  • Any content delivery networks (CDN) for runtime content

All processing engines and libraries used by ToolCEO are bundled locally within the application package itself. No external network call is made at runtime.

Children's Privacy

COPPA Compliant

ToolCEO complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent international regulations protecting minors' privacy.

Because ToolCEO collects absolutely no personal information from any user — regardless of age — it is inherently safe for use by children and does not require parental consent for data collection purposes.

ToolCEO does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13, or from users of any age. No registration, account creation, or personal information is ever requested or required.

If you are a parent or guardian with concerns about your child's use of ToolCEO, you may review this Privacy Policy in full. If you believe that your child has somehow provided us with personal information (which is not possible through the application's design), please contact us at the address listed below and we will address your concerns promptly.
